# QuotePilot — Direct Acquisition Brief

## Offer

**QuotePilot** is a browser-first estimating and quote workflow prototype for small contractors and service businesses.

- Indicative asking price: **US$1,500 one-time**, open to a serious cash offer. This is a seller ask, not an independent valuation.
- Demo: https://dmytri595-design.github.io/quote-pilot/
- Source: https://github.com/dmytri595-design/quote-pilot
- Stage: pre-launch / pre-revenue. No customers, revenue, or real business metrics are claimed.

## What is built

- Dashboard and quote pipeline.
- Quote creation with editable line items, quantities, rates, discounts, taxes, subtotals, and totals.
- Customer records and reusable service/pricing catalog.
- Quote detail workflow from draft to sent, accepted, and invoiced.
- Invoice register derived from accepted/invoiced quotes, with duplicate invoice prevention.
- Customer-facing preview and browser print / Save as PDF.
- Local JSON backup and restore with input validation.
- Analytics based on local demonstration data.
- Adapter contracts for future AI drafting, email delivery, payments, and persistent storage.
- Static GitHub Pages build and Playwright smoke tests.

## Explicit limitations

The product is a browser-local prototype. AI quote drafting, email sending, and payments use mock adapters. No real email is sent, no payment is processed, and no external AI provider is called. Authentication, hosted database, team access controls, accounting integrations, and server-side PDF generation are not implemented. Demo customers, figures, and sample jobs are illustrative.
